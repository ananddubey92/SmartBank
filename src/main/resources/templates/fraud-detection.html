Multi-Vector Rule Matrix
5 Core Vectors

Deterministic vectors evaluate user behavioral history and accumulate calibrated risk points before ML ensemble blending:

1. Historical Amount Multiplier +25 to +40 Pts

Evaluates current amount against 30-day historical average. >3× adds +25 pts; >5× adds +40 pts.

2. High-Frequency Velocity Spike +25 Pts

Scans the transaction ledger. >3 transfers initiated from the debit account within 10 minutes triggers velocity penalties.

3. Beneficiary Novelty Window +20 Pts

Recipients registered within the last 24 hours or with 0 prior completed transfers trigger novelty step-up checks.

4. Temporal Overnight Anomaly +15 Pts

Transactions initiated during non-business overnight hours (00:00 AM – 05:00 AM) receive overnight timing flags.

5. Balance Depletion Ratio (>85%) +20 Pts

Transfers that deplete more than 85% of available account balance in a single execution trigger depletion anomaly warnings.
